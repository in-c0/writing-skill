What to compare

Do not ask only which version is more polished. Look at clarity, naturalness, usefulness, voice preservation, reader effort, concreteness, recoverability, rhetorical over-engineering, rhythm, genre fit, and whether you would actually keep reading.

Experimental detail: Draft → review starts from the exact Baseline output generated in the same run. The model and decoding settings stay fixed; the prompting workflow is what changes.

Model: SmolLM2-360M-Instruct ONNX, quantized for in-browser inference with Transformers.js.
