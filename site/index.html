Unedited outputs. Marks show surface patterns such as em dashes, triads and stock words; they are hints, not proof.

Judge three rounds

Four anonymous drafts per round. Pick the best and the worst; your picks can be added to the human column of the results.

Use it

In an agent

$ npx skills add in-c0/writing-skill --skill storyscope -g

Swap storyscope for writing-skill-storyscope or writing-skill. Drop -g to install into one project.

In the Claude app

Download storyscope.zip and upload it under Skills in settings. Also available: writing-skill-storyscope.zip, writing-skill.zip.

In any chat

Paste the prompt, then add your task after the last line.

StoryScope comes from Russell, Rajendhran, Pham, Iyyer and Wieting (2026), a study of long-form fiction by humans and five LLMs (arXiv:2604.03136, data under MIT). Using it for blog posts and sales copy is this project's own extrapolation. This is a small pilot: one writing model, twelve judgements per setup, judges from the same model family. Every draft, score and script is on GitHub.
