# AI-fiction defaults, measured

Source: StoryScope feature table (61,575 stories: 10,239 human, ~51,300 from GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3 Flash, DeepSeek V3.2, Kimi K2.5; same prompts). Computed by `scripts/derive_defaults.py` from `storyscope_features.parquet`.

Each row: the value AI stories pick far more often than human ones (the **AI default**), and the value human stories pick more often (the **human move**). `gap` is total-variation distance between the AI and human distributions for that feature (0 = identical, 1 = disjoint). Rows are sorted by gap.

Use this as a map of where AI fiction collapses into one region, not as a list of banned moves. Any single row is a legitimate craft choice; the tell is hitting most of them at once.
