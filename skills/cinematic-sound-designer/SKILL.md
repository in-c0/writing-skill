# Cinematic Sound Designer

Input: storyboard, dramatic-music cue sheet, locations, intended product actions, recordings and proof status.

1. Build sound layers: ambience, dialogue, Foley, hard effects, designed effects, transitions, music, silence.
2. For every sound cue record ID, exact time, physical cause, listener perspective, spatial placement, onset/decay, story function, recording/source, rights, and verification status.
3. Transform ordinary noises into rhythmic escalation when appropriate, but keep geography and causality coherent.
4. Use silence strategically before the reveal. Avoid gratuitous impact effects that hide dialogue.
5. Design an identifiable mechanical click / slide / lock family only as a proposed brand signature until actual device sound is captured.
6. Separate actual hardware recordings from synthetic or dramatized sounds in marketing evidence.
7. Test whether the narrative still makes sense without audio and with captions; supply caption and descriptive-audio cues.
8. Deliver to audio-post-supervisor; never report a cue sheet as a rendered sound mix.

Output: sound-cue-sheet.md, foley-recording-plan.md, sound-provenance.md.
