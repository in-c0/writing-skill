# Audio Post Supervisor

Input: timecoded storyboard, music and SFX cue sheets, actual media assets, target platform delivery specification.

1. Validate source filenames, duration, sample rates, rights and provenance; reject missing or unlicensed sources.
2. Organize dialogue/VO, music, ambience, Foley, designed FX stems. Establish dialogue priority and planned ducking.
3. Measure integrated and short-term loudness, true peak, clipping, channel balance, and sync against the actual platform specification; do not invent universal LUFS targets.
4. Listen on headphones, phone speaker and laptop/mono downmix. Test critical reveals and speech intelligibility.
5. Verify sound transitions, deliberate silence, no accidental dropouts, and subtitles/accessible alternate.
6. Record observed measurement values, tools, files, timestamps and independent reviewer findings.
7. Statuses: PLAN ONLY → ASSETS PRESENT → RENDERED → MEASURED → LISTENED → APPROVED. Never skip or infer a stage.
8. Public release requires /slop-gate and actual audiovisual review; if blocked, report unverified and do not publish.

Output: mix-qc.md, delivery-spec.md, rights-ledger.md.
