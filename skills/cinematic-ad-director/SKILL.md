# Cinematic Ad Director

## Purpose
Turn an everyday frustration into an entertaining, tense, surprising commercial that communicates a product's distinct value without unsupported claims. This skill was distilled from a CuffA Kickstarter ideation process; it is reusable for other products.

## Core workflow
1. **Reality and claims**: inventory what is demonstrated, proposed, or unknown. Mark each shot VERIFIED, PROTOTYPE, CONCEPT, or UNKNOWN. Never disguise concept VFX as real product demonstration.
2. **Friction mining**: generate at least 12 ordinary situations where a familiar tool becomes cumbersome. Score each by recognizability, visual clarity, severity, product differentiation, and production feasibility. Distinguish existing alternatives (e.g. smartwatch glance) from a genuinely unsolved need.
3. **Causal escalation**: select one protagonist, one immediate objective and one escalating chain. Each inconvenience must result from the previous decision, not merely appear in a montage. Map action → consequence → worse constraint.
4. **Surprise engineering**: design at least three structurally different reversals: expectation inversion, role reversal, and recontextualization. For each write audience belief → evidence → reveal → consequence. Reject arbitrary twists that don't change the story.
5. **Product as payoff**: remove the product from the draft. If the plot still resolves identically, revise. Make the reveal solve a specific established problem while respecting real ergonomic limitations.
6. **Storyboard**: deliver shot ID, start/end seconds, action, frame/lens, blocking, camera movement, dialogue, sound cue, viewer belief, twist, product visibility, proof status, and transition. Ensure total duration equals requested runtime.
7. **Audio integration**: invoke dramatic-music-director, cinematic-sound-designer and audio-post-supervisor as needed; audio can foreshadow, misdirect, pause, or deliver the reveal.
8. **Independent adversarial review**: separate story reviewer, buyer-comprehension reviewer and claims/production reviewer. Explicitly flag unverified review, not PASS.
9. **Variants and evidence**: make 6–15s hook, 30s cutdown, 60–120s hero version as requested. Specify A/B hypotheses and outcome metrics (qualified interest, recall, intent), not just views.
10. **Release gate**: run repository /slop-gate if available; inspect rendered deliverables and real playback, licensing and claims. If unavailable, report NOT VERIFIED and do not say released.

## Working principles
- Start from an observed human inconvenience, not a feature list.
- Surprise must be caused, not random; rhythm alternates pressure, silence, laughter and release.
- An exciting scene is not automatically a persuasive ad. Evaluate whether the viewer understands why this product rather than a phone, watch or other existing solution.
- Distinguish screenplay, concept art, filmed evidence, and shipping product.
- Use existing writing-skill and StoryScope selectively; their experimental benchmark is not evidence of ad conversion uplift.
- Prefer actual footage and truthful annotations for hardware crowdfunding.
- Preserve the user's original creative intent in any PR or issue description.
