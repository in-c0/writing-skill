# The Laneway Pen

A stainless-steel fountain pen you refill instead of replacing.

The Laneway Pen is made by two people in a small workshop in Melbourne. It costs AUD 140, weighs 32 g, and is built to be the pen you keep using after the cheap ones have gone in the bin. If you've wanted to try a fountain pen but weren't sure about the upkeep, this page covers the parts that usually worry people.

## Ink you can find anywhere

The Laneway takes standard international cartridges, the size most stationers and online shops already stock. If you'd rather use bottled ink, fit a converter and fill it from whatever bottle you like. You can switch between cartridges and bottled ink whenever you want, so running out halfway through a notebook is easy to fix.

## A steel body at 32 g

Stainless steel gives the pen more weight than a plastic one, and you'll notice it sitting in your hand. At 32 g it still isn't heavy, so a long page of notes or a stack of cards is comfortable to get through. The body won't crack if it rolls off a desk or rattles around loose in a bag.

## A nib replacement promise for life

The nib is the part of a fountain pen most likely to need attention, usually after a drop or years of pressing hard. Every Laneway Pen comes with a lifetime nib-replacement promise. If your nib wears out or gets damaged, contact us, tell us what happened, and we'll replace it.
