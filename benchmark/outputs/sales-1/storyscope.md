# The Laneway Pen

**A refillable stainless-steel fountain pen, made in Melbourne. AUD 140.**

We're a workshop of two in Melbourne, so whoever answers your email probably had a hand in making your pen. The Laneway is the pen we wanted for ourselves: plain steel, a nib that writes from the first line, and nothing that needs babying. Throw it in a bag with your keys. It'll be fine.

## Ink from anywhere

It takes standard international cartridges, the kind most stationers and plenty of newsagents keep on the shelf, so you're never stuck waiting on us for refills. Prefer bottled ink? Fit a converter and fill it from whatever bottle you like. Bottled is cheaper per page anyway, and the colour range is enormous.

## 32 grams

That's roughly five $2 coins. You'll feel some weight when you pick it up, which is the point of steel. Still, we've filled a few notebooks each testing it, and nobody's wrist has complained yet.

## A nib for life

The nib does all the work, and it's also the part most likely to get hurt. So for as long as you own the pen, we'll replace the nib whenever it needs it. Email us, tell us what happened, and we'll take it from there. Embarrassing stories welcome but not required.
