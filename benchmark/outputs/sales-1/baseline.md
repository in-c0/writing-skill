# The Laneway Pen

**Steel, ink, and a promise that outlasts the nib.**

The Laneway Pen comes from a two-person workshop in Melbourne. It's a refillable stainless-steel fountain pen for the lists, letters and margin notes you write every day. It isn't meant to be thrown away. You fill it, write with it, and keep it.

## Made to be refilled, not replaced

The Laneway takes standard international cartridges, which you can buy almost anywhere. If you prefer bottled ink, fit a converter and use any colour you like. Either way, you only ever replace the ink.

## Weighted for the long page

At 32 g, it's heavy enough to sit steady in your hand but light enough for a full page without tiring. The stainless-steel body copes with the bottom of a bag, the edge of a desk and years of daily use.

## A nib for life

Nibs wear down, bend, and sometimes hit the floor tip-first. When that happens to yours, we'll replace it. The promise has no time limit: it lasts as long as the pen does. We built the Laneway to last, and we'd rather post you a new nib than see the pen left in a drawer.
