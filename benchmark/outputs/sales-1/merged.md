# The Laneway Pen

A refillable stainless-steel fountain pen, made by the two of us in a small Melbourne workshop. AUD 140.

If you've been writing with whatever pen happened to be next to the phone, this is a good place to stop. We made the Laneway to be used every day: clipped in a jacket pocket, left on the desk, picked up again for the shopping list or the meeting notes or the birthday card you meant to write last week.

## Easy to fill

The Laneway takes standard international cartridges, the kind most stationery and pen shops sell. If you'd rather use bottled ink, fit a converter and fill it from any bottle you like. You can switch between the two whenever you want, so trying a new colour doesn't mean buying a new pen.

## Light in the hand

Stainless steel sounds heavy, but the Laneway weighs 32 g. That's enough to feel like a proper object when you hold it, and light enough that a few pages of notes won't leave your hand tired.

## A nib for life

Nibs wear in over the years, and now and then one gets bent. Pens get dropped, sat on and borrowed by people who press too hard. That's why every Laneway comes with a lifetime nib-replacement promise. If something happens to your nib, tell us and we'll replace it. You don't have to explain how the cat was involved.
